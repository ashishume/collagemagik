# Motion Templates — ElevenLabs v4 voiceover scripts

Two short ads (9:16, Reels / Shorts / TikTok / App campaigns) for the Motion templates feature.
Audio tags in `[brackets]` are ElevenLabs v4 delivery tags: paste the **TTS block** as is.
Avoid "Insta" anywhere (Meta brand rule).

---

## Ad 1 — "Make it move" (15 s, high-energy hype)

**Vibe:** neon, punchy, beat-synced cuts. Uses the *Neon nights* template (full-screen photo → slide up → zoom → 3-up pop).
**Voice:** young, confident, upbeat (e.g. a bright hype voice). Stability low (~0.3), style high.
**Music:** 120–128 BPM electro-pop, a drop on "Boom".

### TTS block (paste into ElevenLabs)

```
[excited] Your photos? Kinda... still. [short pause]
[energetic] Not anymore.
Pick a motion template... drop in your photos AND videos... [snaps fingers]
Boom! [laughs] They zoom, they slide, they pop, and your clips play right inside.
[confident] Collage Magic. Make your memories move.
```

### Shot list

| Time | Voiceover | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 s | "Your photos? Kinda… still." | A flat, static photo grid. Slight desaturation. |
| 2.0–3.0 s | "Not anymore." | Grid shatters; neon glow sweeps in. |
| 3.0–6.5 s | "Pick a motion template… drop in your photos AND videos…" | Thumb taps the *Neon nights* card (sparkles badge), picks 2 photos + 1 video (video badge visible in picker). |
| 6.5–11.5 s | "Boom! They zoom, they slide, they pop, and your clips play right inside." | Template plays: zoom-in → slide-up → zoom → 3-up pop, cut to the beat; the video clip is visibly playing in its cell. |
| 11.5–15.0 s | "Collage Magic. Make your memories move." | App icon + "Collage Magic" + App Store badge. Text: **Photos + videos. 18 free motion templates.** |

## Ad 2 — "Weekend in 10 seconds" (15 s, playful & warm → payoff)

**Vibe:** sunny, friendly, a little cheeky. Uses *Photo dump* / *Weekend* / *Print drop* templates with a video clip playing inside.
**Voice:** warm, smiling, conversational. Stability ~0.4, style medium-high.
**Music:** bouncy indie-pop, claps; a riser into the last line.

### TTS block (paste into ElevenLabs)

```
[playful] Three hundred photos from one weekend? [laughs] Same.
[curious] Here's the trick...
[excited] Tap a motion template, mix your photos AND videos, and watch them glide into place, all by themselves!
[warm] Your videos keep playing inside. No editing skills. No stress.
[cheerful] Collage Magic. Your weekend, in motion.
```

### Shot list

| Time | Voiceover | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 s | "Three hundred photos from one weekend? Same." | Camera roll scrolling fast; a relatable sigh emoji. |
| 2.5–3.5 s | "Here's the trick…" | Finger hovers over the Collage tab → Motion row. |
| 3.5–9.0 s | "Tap a motion template, mix your photos AND videos… all by themselves!" | Picker shows photos and videos selected together. *Photo dump* plays: prints drop and pop in, a video clip plays inside one cell. Quick cut to *Print drop*. |
| 9.0–11.5 s | "Your videos keep playing inside. No editing skills. No stress." | Close-up on a playing video cell, then one-tap export → saved to Photos. |
| 11.5–15.0 s | "Collage Magic. Your weekend, in motion." | App icon + App Store badge. Text: **Photos + videos. Free motion templates.** |

## Notes

- Each script is ~35–40 words, so it lands at ~13–15 s at a natural pace. If ElevenLabs reads long, cut the second
  sentence of the middle line first.
- Want 6 s bumpers? Use only the last two lines of either script.
- Feature claims used here are all true in the app: 18 free motion templates, photos and videos play inside the
  design, scenes zoom / slide / pop, export straight to Photos.
